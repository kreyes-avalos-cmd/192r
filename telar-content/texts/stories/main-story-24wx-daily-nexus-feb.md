---
title: "Daily Nexus feb. 10,1993"
---

The Santa Barbara High School District was approached by the AIDS Education program, which visited different schools doing assemblies in order ot spread awareness of the AIDS Epidemic.  This assembly proposed was a theater performance sponsored by the Kaiser Permanente Health Group. Word of this news caused pushback from parents. As a response, this assembly required a permission slip to be signed by parents in order to attend.
